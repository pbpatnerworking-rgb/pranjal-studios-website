Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026 | Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At PRANJAL STUDIOS (operated by developer Pranjal Tiwari), accessible from https://pranjal-studios.in, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and application users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by PRANJAL STUDIOS and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Our Android applications—including Kids Learning Games, STDHELP Smart Study, Dimag Ka Dahi Quiz, and Bubble Galaxy Legends—are designed with privacy-first principles:

  • Personal Identifiable Data: We do NOT require or force users to register account credentials or provide full real names to use basic app functions.
  • Form Submissions: If you use our website contact, bug report, or feature request forms, we collect your provided email address, device model, and message strictly to respond to your support request.
  • App Usage & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs and basic device performance metrics may be processed by Google Play Console Services to fix software bugs and improve app stability.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Developer Program Policies concerning child safety:

  • Our children's apps (such as Kids Learning Games) DO NOT request, collect, store, or transmit personal data from children under 13 years of age.
  • No targeted or personalized behavioral advertising is served in apps directed at children.
  • In-app feedback or external contact buttons in child-directed apps are protected behind adult parental gates.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Certain apps may display non-personalized contextual advertisements via Google AdMob or use official Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. These services operate under Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • AdMob Advertising Policies

4. Data Storage & Security

User preferences, study timers, game scores, and local progress in our mobile applications are stored locally on your device storage (via SharedPreferences / local database). Clearing application data or uninstalling the app removes this data from your phone.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to or deletion of any email support communications sent to us. To exercise your rights, please contact developer Pranjal Tiwari at stdhelp.support@gmail.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at:
