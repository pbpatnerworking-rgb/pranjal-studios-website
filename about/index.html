Building Helpful Apps for Everyday Life

PRANJAL STUDIOS was founded by Pranjal Tiwari with a singular mission: to design and build high-quality Android applications that combine beautiful user interfaces with utility, privacy, and speed.

Our product lineup spans early childhood educational tools (Kids Learning Games), smart study assistants (STDHELP Smart Study), brain trivia games (Dimag Ka Dahi Quiz), arcade games (Bubble Galaxy Legends), and AI utilities (ScanMaster AI).
